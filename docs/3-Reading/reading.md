The IELTS Reading test evaluates your ability to understand academic or general English texts. It tests:

-   Reading speed
-   Vocabulary knowledge
-   Understanding of arguments and opinions
-   Ability to locate specific information
-   Logical reasoning

There are **two versions**:

-   **Academic**
-   **General Training**

# 1. Test Format

## ⏰ Time

-   60 minutes
-   40 questions
-   No extra time to transfer answers

You must write answers directly on the answer sheet.

## 📚 Academic Reading

-   3 long passages
-   Total about **2,150–2,750** words
-   Topics: science, history, sociology, environment, psychology, etc.
-   Texts are from books, journals, newspapers

Difficulty increases from Passage 1 → Passage 3.

## 📄 General Training Reading

-   Section 1: Short factual texts (ads, notices)
-   Section 2: Workplace-related texts
-   Section 3: One long text (similar to Academic but slightly easier)

# 2. Question Types (Very Important)

IELTS Reading has many question types. You must understand each type clearly.

## 1. Multiple Choice

Choose A, B, C (sometimes D).

🔹 Strategy:

-   Read the question first
-   Underline keywords
-   Find paraphrases in the passage
-   Eliminate wrong options

## 2. True / False / Not Given (Academic)

Or Yes / No / Not Given (opinion-based)

This is one of the hardest types.

-   **TRUE** = matches the passage
-   **FALSE** = contradicts the passage
-   **NOT GIVEN** = information not mentioned

🔹 Common mistake:

Students confuse FALSE and NOT GIVEN.

Ask yourself:

>   Does the passage clearly say the opposite? → FALSE
>
>   Or does it not talk about this information? → NOT GIVEN

## 3. Matching Headings

Match headings to paragraphs.

🔹 Strategy:

-   Do this first (while brain is fresh)
-   Focus on the main idea of each paragraph
-   Ignore details

## 4. Sentence Completion

Complete sentences with words from the passage.

Rules:

-   Usually NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS
-   Check grammar carefully

## 5. Summary Completion

Complete a summary using:

-   Words from passage
-   Word list

Focus on:

-   Grammar
-   Logical meaning
-   Synonyms

## 6. Matching Information

Match statements to paragraphs.

You must scan carefully. Information may appear anywhere.

## 7. Diagram / Flowchart Completion

Usually easier if vocabulary is simple.

# 3. Essential Reading Skills

IELTS Reading is NOT normal reading. It is strategic reading.

## 🔎 1. Skimming

Reading quickly to understand:

-   Main idea
-   Topic
-   Structure

When to use:

-   First reading of passage
-   Matching headings

## 🎯 2. Scanning

Reading quickly to find:

-   Names
-   Dates
-   Numbers
-   Keywords

Very important for:

-   True/False/Not Given
-   Sentence completion

## 🧠 3. Understanding Paraphrasing

IELTS NEVER copies the exact sentence.

Example:

Passage:

>   The experiment was **conducted** in **1995**.

Question:

>   The research **took place** in the **mid-1990s**.

Same meaning, different words.

You must train your brain to recognize synonyms.

# 4. Time Management Strategy (Critical)

60 minutes = very limited time.

Recommended plan:

-   Passage 1 → 15–18 minutes
-   Passage 2 → 20 minutes
-   Passage 3 → 22–25 minutes

If stuck:

👉 Move on immediately

👉 Come back later

Never spend 5 minutes on one question.

# 5. Step-by-Step Method for Each Passage

Here is a practical method:

### Step 1: Read the Title

Understand topic.

### Step 2: Skim the Whole Passage (3–4 minutes)

-   Read first sentence of each paragraph
-   Identify structure

### Step 3: Read Questions Carefully

-   Underline keywords
-   Identify question type

### Step 4: Locate Keywords

Scan passage.

### Step 5: Read Carefully Around Keywords

Check meaning carefully.

# 6. Common Mistakes

❌ Reading word by word from beginning

❌ Spending too long on difficult question

❌ Ignoring word limit

❌ Spelling mistakes

❌ Not checking grammar in completion tasks

Remember:

IELTS is NOT testing your knowledge.

It tests your reading strategy.

# 7. How to Practice Effectively

Since you are preparing for IELTS, here is a focused plan:

### Daily Training (1–2 hours)

1.  Do one passage under time limit.
2.  Check answers carefully.
3.  Analyze:
    -   Why wrong?
    -   Which keywords missed?
    -   Which paraphrases not recognized?
4.  Write down new vocabulary in a notebook.
5.  Review mistakes weekly.

## Improve Vocabulary for Reading

Focus on:

-   Academic words (**AWL list**)
-   Synonyms
-   Word families

Example:

-   analyze → analysis → analytical
-   economy → economic → economical

# 8. Band Score Requirements

Approximate Academic Reading scores:

-   30/40 → Band 7
-   35/40 → Band 8
-   37–40 → Band 9

If your target is Band 7+, accuracy must be high.

# 9. Advanced Tips (For Band 7+)

✔️ Understand writer’s attitude

✔️ Identify **argument structure**

✔️ Recognize contrast words:

-   however
-   although
-   whereas
-   on the contrary

✔️ Pay attention to:

-   Reference words (this, that, they, such)

# 10. Final Advice

IELTS Reading is:

>   40% strategy
>
>   30% vocabulary
>
>   20% speed
>
>   10% concentration

If you train correctly for 6–8 weeks, big improvement is possible.
