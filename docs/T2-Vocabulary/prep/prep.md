# Prepositions

## 🗺️ Prepositions of Place and Position

These prepositions indicate **where** something is located.

| **Preposition**   | **Explanation**                                              | **Example Sentence**                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **At**            | Used for a **specific point** or location (like a school, or a meeting). | The concert will take place **at** the National Stadium.     |
| **In**            | Used for an **enclosed space** (rooms, buildings, cars, countries, cities). | I enjoy reading the newspaper **in** the morning.            |
| **On**            | Used for surfaces or something attached to a line (street, river). | The book is **on** the shelf. / The house is **on** Main Street. |
| **Above**         | Higher than something else, but **not touching** it.         | A thick layer of clouds was floating **above** the city.     |
| **Over**          | Similar to *above*, often implying movement or covering.     | The bridge stretches **over** the river. / He put a blanket **over** the child. |
| **Below**         | Lower than something else.                                   | The temperature fell **below** freezing last night.          |
| **Under**         | Directly lower than something else (often obscured by it).   | The cat is hiding **under** the bed.                         |
| **Between**       | In the space separating two distinct things.                 | The bank is situated **between** the bookstore and the pharmacy. |
| **Among**         | In a group of three or more things.                          | She was the fastest runner **among** the competitors.        |
| **Near** / **By** | Close to a person or thing.                                  | The library is very **near** my apartment.                   |

## ⏳ Prepositions of Time

These prepositions indicate **when** something happens.

| **Preposition** | **Explanation**                                              | **Example Sentence**                                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **At**          | Used for a **specific time** (clock time, holidays).         | The meeting is scheduled to start **at** 10:30 AM.           |
| **In**          | Used for **longer periods** (months, seasons, years, decades). | Life changed dramatically **in** the 21st century.           |
| **On**          | Used for **specific days** or dates.                         | I have a presentation **on** Friday. / The event is **on** October 15th. |
| **Since**       | From a **specific time in the past** until now.              | I have been studying English **since** 2020.                 |
| **For**         | Over a **duration** of time.                                 | We have been waiting **for** two hours.                      |
| **During**      | Throughout the entire course of an action or event.          | Many people watch television **during** dinner.              |
| **Before**      | Earlier than a specific point in time or event.              | Please submit your essay **before** the deadline.            |
| **After**       | Later than a specific point in time or event.                | I will call you **after** I finish my work.                  |

## 💡 Prepositions of Direction/Movement

These prepositions indicate **how** something moves or is directed.

| **Preposition** | **Explanation**                                              | **Example Sentence**                                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **To**          | Shows movement towards a person or place (the destination).  | She went **to** the market to buy vegetables.                |
| **Into**        | Shows movement from outside to inside an enclosed space.     | He quickly walked **into** the room when it started raining. |
| **Out of**      | Shows movement from inside to outside.                       | The students filed **out of** the lecture hall.              |
| **Through**     | Shows movement from one side to the other of an enclosed area or obstacle. | The path leads **through** the dense forest.                 |
| **Across**      | Shows movement from one side to the opposite side of a flat surface (road, field, river). | We had to swim **across** the lake.                          |
| **Along**       | Shows movement in a line next to something long and narrow (road, river). | They walked **along** the beach at sunset.                   |
| **Towards**     | Shows movement in the **direction** of something (but doesn't necessarily reach it). | The dog ran **towards** its owner, wagging its tail.         |

## 🛠️ Other Common Prepositions

| **Preposition** | **Explanation**                                              | **Example Sentence**                                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Of**          | Indicates a relationship between two nouns, possession, or a part of a whole. | This is the capital city **of** the province. / A cup **of** tea. |
| **With**        | Indicates accompaniment or using a tool/instrument.          | She spoke **with** confidence. / I opened the package **with** a knife. |
| **By**          | Indicates the agent/doer in a passive sentence, or a means of transport/communication. | The novel was written **by** a famous author. / I travel **by** bus. |
| **For**         | Indicates purpose, reason, or recipient.                     | This gift is **for** my mother. / I am studying **for** the exam. |
| **About**       | Indicates the topic or subject matter.                       | The documentary is **about** climate change.                 |
