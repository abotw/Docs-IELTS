## 1. What *is* 《牛津初阶英汉双解词典》?

**《牛津初阶英汉双解词典》** (“Oxford Elementary Learner’s English–Chinese Dictionary”) is an English–Chinese bilingual learner’s dictionary designed specifically for **beginner and elementary English learners**. It provides **clear English definitions and Chinese explanations** side-by-side, helping learners understand both form *and* meaning. ([ci.nii.ac.jp](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA72446472?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

It is part of the **Oxford bilingual dictionary series** published with collaboration from **牛津大学出版社 (Oxford University Press)** and often distributed in China by **商务印书馆 (The Commercial Press)**. ([ci.nii.ac.jp](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA72446472?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## 2. Who is it *for*?

This dictionary is especially suitable for:

-   **Beginners and elementary English learners**
-   **Primary and lower-secondary students**
-   Learners who are transitioning from basic vocabulary to more comprehensive usage
-   Self-learners who need **clear explanations plus Chinese translation** to reinforce understanding ([oupchina.com.hk](https://www.oupchina.com.hk/zh/dictionaries/ec-bilingual?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Unlike **advanced learner dictionaries** which contain very detailed meanings and usages (e.g., 针对大学生或进阶学习者的《牛津高阶英汉双解词典》), this edition keeps definitions **simple and learner-friendly**. ([維基百科](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Advanced_Learner's_Dictionary?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
