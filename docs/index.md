## 1. 核心词汇与短语 (Vocabulary)

- **[100 Essential Words and Phrases for Band 7-9 Success](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/100-Essential-Words-and-Phrases-for-Band-7-9-Success-2.pdf)** (PDF)
    
    - 雅思高分（7-9分）必备的100个核心词汇与短语，适合冲刺提分。
        
- **[IELTS Band 9 Vocab Secrets](https://bayanebartar.org/file-dl/library/IELTS1/IELTS_Band9_VocabSecrets.pdf)** (PDF)
    
    - 满分词汇秘籍：涵盖雅思各主题的高级表达方式。
        
- **[Commonly Used Words in IELTS Listening Test](https://ieltsimmigration.ir/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/1200_most_commonly_repeated_words_in.pdf)** (PDF)
    
    - 雅思听力高频词汇 1200 词，针对听力填空题的核心词库。
