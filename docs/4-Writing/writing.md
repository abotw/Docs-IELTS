The IELTS Writing test is different in **Academic** and **General Training** modules.

-   ⏱ Total time: **60 minutes**
-   ✍️ Total tasks: **2**
-   📊 Task 2 counts **twice as much** as Task 1

# 🏫 IELTS Academic Writing

## ✏️ Task 1 (20 minutes, 150+ words)

You must describe **visual** information.

### Common Question Types

1.  Line graph
2.  Bar chart
3.  Pie chart
4.  Table
5.  Process diagram
6.  Map

### What You Must Do

-   Summarize key features
-   Compare important data
-   Report trends
-   Avoid giving opinions

### Structure (Very Important)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction**

-   Paraphrase the question

**Paragraph 2 – Overview**

-   Summarize **main trends** (most important part!)

**Paragraph 3 – Details 1**

-   Key comparisons + data

**Paragraph 4 – Details 2**

-   Additional comparisons + data

### 💡 Key Skills for Task 1

-   Paraphrasing
-   Comparing data
-   Describing trends
-   Grouping information logically

### Useful Vocabulary

**Trends**

-   increase / decrease
-   rise / fall
-   fluctuate
-   remain stable
-   peak at
-   reach a low

**Comparisons**

-   higher than
-   lower than
-   similar to
-   in contrast
-   whereas

## ✏️ Task 2 (40 minutes, 250+ words)

You must write an essay.

This is the most important part.

### Common Essay Types

1.  Opinion (Agree/Disagree)
2.  Discussion (Discuss both views)
3.  Problem & Solution
4.  Advantages & Disadvantages
5.  Double question

### Standard Essay Structure (Band 7+ Structure)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction**

-   Paraphrase
-   Thesis statement (your position clearly)

**Paragraph 2 – Main Idea 1**

-   Topic sentence
-   Explanation
-   Example
-   Mini conclusion

**Paragraph 3 – Main Idea 2**

-   Topic sentence
-   Explanation
-   Example
-   Mini conclusion

**Paragraph 4 – Conclusion**

-   Restate position
-   Summarize key ideas

# 🎯 IELTS Writing Band Descriptors (How You Are Marked)

You are scored in **4 categories**:

## 1️⃣ Task Response

-   Did you answer ALL parts?
-   Is your position clear?
-   Are ideas fully developed?

## 2️⃣ Coherence & Cohesion

-   Logical paragraphing
-   Clear progression
-   Linking words used naturally

## 3️⃣ Lexical Resource

-   Vocabulary range
-   Accurate word choice
-   Less repetition

## 4️⃣ Grammatical Range & Accuracy

-   Sentence variety
-   Complex structures
-   Few errors

# 🔥 What Examiners Really Want (Band 7–8 Strategy)

Many students misunderstand this part.

Examiners prefer:

✔ Clear position

✔ Logical development

✔ Simple but correct grammar

✔ Natural vocabulary

✔ No memorized templates

They DO NOT require:

❌ Extremely advanced vocabulary

❌ Very complicated ideas

❌ Academic research knowledge

Clarity > Complexity.

# ⏰ Time Management Strategy (Recommended)

### Academic Writing

**Task 1**

-   3 min analyse
-   2 min plan
-   15 min write

**Task 2**

-   5 min analyse
-   5 min plan
-   30 min write

# 🧠 High-Level Strategy for Band 7+

## 1️⃣ Think Before Writing

Planning improves coherence and vocabulary control.

## 2️⃣ One Clear Idea Per Paragraph

Avoid mixing too many ideas.

## 3️⃣ Explain, Don’t List

Bad:

>   There are many advantages. First, money. Second, education. Third, freedom.

Good:

>   One significant advantage is financial independence, which allows individuals to make personal decisions without relying on family support.

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Very Important)

1.  Writing too few words
2.  Memorized essays
3.  Overusing linking words (Firstly, Moreover, Furthermore…)
4.  Writing informal language
5.  Not answering all parts of the question
6.  Writing conclusion too short

# 📈 How You Should Practice (Especially for You)

Since you're preparing seriously for IELTS, here’s a strategic plan:

### Week Plan

-   2 Task 1 practices
-   2 Task 2 essays
-   1 vocabulary review session
-   1 grammar correction session

### Advanced Practice Method

1.  Write essay
2.  Wait 24 hours
3.  Rewrite from memory
4.  Compare versions
5.  Improve structure

This method dramatically improves band score.

# 🧩 Advanced Writing Tips

### Use Topic-Specific Vocabulary

Education topic → curriculum, academic performance, tuition fees

Environment topic → carbon emissions, renewable energy

### Use Flexible Sentence Structures

-   Relative clauses
-   Conditionals
-   Passive voice
-   Concession clauses (Although, While)

# 🎓 Final Advice for Band 7–8

If your goal is Band 7+:

-   Prioritize Task 2
-   Focus on clarity
-   Improve grammar accuracy
-   Avoid over-complication
-   Practice timed writing
