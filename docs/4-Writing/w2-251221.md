## "Agree or Disagree" regarding globalization

### The Question

> The spread of multinational companies and the resulting increase of globalization produce positive effects for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### **Model Answer**

The world is moving in a direction where the tentacles of cross-national corporations are reaching to all corners of the world. While this trend has been applauded by the devotees of globalization as a blessing for everyone, I will say it is a generalized opinion ignoring the discrete interests of individuals.

There seem to be reasons why the prosperity of multinational companies and the ensuing globalization is assumed beneficial to all. Those who think in this way may point to legions of advantages in daily life, whether these be job opportunities, equal accessibility to the same product from any corner of the world, or the easy-to-reach after-sales service wherever they go. Examples regarding these include international brands such as Dell, Apple, and McDonald's, all of which play a key role in employment and their products are becoming an imperative part in lives of various demographics. Given this, here comes the assumption that the populace is the whole beneficiary of a globalized economy.

Plausible though it seems, it is difficult for me to see the veneration of a globalized business world as a blessing to all. Those who focus on its positive side of this trend are oblivious to the fact that the ramifications of any social episodes should be domain-specific and context-dependent. Based on this, the discussion can then move on to hard questions as to which social impacts are more desirable in the local context or what local stakeholders value more. From the perspective of local industries in developing countries, the relentless invasion of international brands may oust them from the local market. To complicate matters even more, the disappearance of indigenous artefacts may jeopardize national identity and cultural diversity in the world.

From what has been discussed above, it is my opinion therefore, the benefits of a globalized economy dominated by multinational companies do not apply to everyone.

### **Key Vocabulary & Expressions to Note**

If you are aiming for a high band score (7.0+), pay attention to these phrases used in the text:

- **Tentacles of cross-national corporations:** A metaphor for the far-reaching influence of big companies.
    
- **Devotees of globalization:** People who strongly support globalization.
    
- **Discrete interests:** Separate or distinct interests.
    
- **Ensuing globalization:** The globalization that follows as a result.
    
- **Legions of advantages:** A large number of benefits.
    
- **Plausible though it seems:** A sophisticated way to say "Even though it sounds reasonable."
    
- **Oblivious to the fact:** Unaware of the truth.
    
- **Jeopardize:** To put something (like national identity) at risk.

### 1. Essay Structure Analysis

This essay follows a **balanced/partial disagreement** approach. It acknowledges the popular view before presenting a strong counter-argument.

- **Introduction:** Introduces the topic using a "Hook" (the spread of corporations) and a clear **Thesis Statement**(disagreeing that it benefits _everyone_).
    
- **Body Paragraph 1 (The Counter-Argument):** Explains _why_ some people think globalization is good. It mentions job opportunities and product accessibility (e.g., Apple, Dell).
    
- **Body Paragraph 2 (The Refutation/Main Argument):** This is the strongest part. It argues that benefits are "context-dependent" and highlights the negative impact on local industries and cultural identity.
    
- **Conclusion:** Reaffirms the position that while globalization has perks, they are not universal.
    

### 2. Vocabulary & Synonyms

To avoid repeating words like "globalization" or "companies," use these alternatives in your writing:

|**Category**|**High-Level Synonyms**|

|**Local/Small Scale**|Indigenous industries, domestic businesses, regional stakeholders, grassroots level.|

### 3. Sentence Structures to Mimic

The model essay uses "Advanced Grammar" which examiners love. Try to practice these two patterns:

1. **The "Inverted" Adjective Clause:**
    
    - _Text:_ "Plausible though it seems..."
        
    - _Your Practice:_ "**Difficult though it may be**, governments must regulate international trade."
        
2. **The "Whether... or..." Structure:**
    
    - _Text:_ "...whether these be job opportunities, equal accessibility... or after-sales service."
        
    - _Your Practice:_ "Globalization affects every citizen, **whether they be** consumers in cities **or** farmers in rural areas."
