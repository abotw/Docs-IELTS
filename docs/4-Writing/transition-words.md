
# Transition Words

## Summary of "Starting" Transitions

|**Category**|**Vocabulary / Phrases**|**Usage Context**|
|---|---|---|
|**Formal & Academic (IELTS Writing)**|_First and foremost, Initially, At the outset, The first point to consider is..._|Use these in **Writing Task 2** to signal your first major argument.|
|**Standard / Sequential**|_To begin/start with, In the first place, First of all_|Great for organizing steps or logical sequences in both writing and speaking.|
|**Time-Related (Past vs. Now)**|_At first, Originally, Back at the beginning_|Best for **Speaking Part 1 or 2** when describing how a situation or feeling changed over time.|

### Quick Tips for your IELTS Exam:

- **For Writing:** Avoid "Back at the beginning" or "First of all"—they are a bit too informal. Stick to **"First and foremost"** or **"To begin with."**
- **For Speaking:** Feel free to use **"At first"** or **"Originally"** when telling a story about a hobby or a place you used to live.
